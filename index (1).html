I'm a senior creative and marketing professional with 8 years of experience helping businesses get noticed and grow. My work spans brand identity, print and digital advertising, paid media, event marketing, and the analytics that tie it all together.

An academic foundation in Psychology shapes how I think about marketing in ways that go well beyond the classroom. Consumer behavior, motivation, attention, and decision-making aren't abstract concepts here; they're the actual mechanics behind what makes a campaign convert or a design stop a scroll. Understanding the why behind human response means I can build work that connects, not just work that looks good.

On the digital side, I've managed Google Ads, Meta Ads, and Pinterest campaigns for clients spanning healthcare, political campaigns, real estate, finance, and consumer brands across multiple industries. That breadth means I understand how differently audiences behave depending on context, and how to build campaigns that speak to them. Results across the board include over 75 million impressions generated and consistently strong conversion performance, all grounded in solid tracking infrastructure: GA4, Google Tag Manager, and custom event setup so every decision is measurable.

My production work covers brand systems, print collateral, large-format graphics, and event materials at any scale. Clients have included defense and military technology organizations with major international trade show presences, alongside local businesses building their brand for the first time. Every deliverable is built to hold up in the real world.
